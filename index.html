Why Choose Campus Market

Traditional marketplaces are plagued by shipping hassles, payment scams, and anonymous sellers. CampusMarket keeps transactions hyper-local and safe.

Verified Student Network

Trade with confidence. All sellers and buyers are fellow university students and staff with recognized campus email domains and designated residence halls.

Zero Shipping Fees

Never wait days for delivery or pay inflated courier charges. Arrange easy physical hand-offs at the university library, cafeteria, or dorm common rooms.

Budget-Friendly & Circular

Senior students recoup costs by passing on required textbooks and dorm appliances to underclassmen, building a sustainable, budget-friendly campus circular economy.
